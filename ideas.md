# Stage and Stay — Design Direction

## Three possible directions

### Theme Name: Editorial Property Journal
Very restrained, gallery-like and architectural, using property photography as the primary storytelling medium.
Probability: 0.07

### Theme Name: Soft Sydney Modern
Warm, residential and approachable, pairing tactile neutrals with a more conversational service experience.
Probability: 0.04

### Theme Name: Quiet Commercial Luxury
A high-trust, premium service aesthetic with dark green anchors, refined typography and precise conversion moments.
Probability: 0.08

## Chosen approach: Editorial Property Journal

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial design inspired by premium property lookbooks, architecture journals and independent interiors publications.

### Core Principles
1. Let real styled spaces lead the story; text should frame the image rather than compete with it.
2. Use asymmetric compositions, generous negative space and thin rules to create a considered rhythm.
3. Pair emotional serif headlines with highly legible sans-serif service copy.
4. Treat the pricing condition as a design feature: transparent, visible and never hidden in fine print.

### Color Philosophy
Warm ivory and sand create a calm residential canvas. Deep green signals trust and seriousness, while muted terracotta marks moments of human warmth and conversion. The palette should feel like a well-lit Sydney home rather than a generic technology product.

### Layout Paradigm
Use a magazine-like flow: oversized hero imagery, split editorial sections, project strips, detail crops and full-width statements. Avoid repetitive centered cards and let each section alternate between image-led and information-led compositions.

### Signature Elements
- Thin charcoal rules and small uppercase section markers.
- Large serif headlines with selective italic emphasis.
- Project captions and detail labels treated like portfolio metadata.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like turning a page: restrained, responsive and purposeful. Navigation anchors should move smoothly, accordions should reveal answers without visual drama, and the consultation form should feel like a quiet continuation of the editorial experience.

### Animation
Use short, low-key reveals for section headings and image crops when they enter the viewport. Use subtle image scale on hover for project images and a quick press response for buttons. Respect prefers-reduced-motion and avoid decorative animation that distracts from the property photography.

### Typography System
Use Cormorant Garamond for display headlines and italic emphasis. Use DM Sans for navigation, body copy, labels and form text. Headlines should be large and airy; labels should be uppercase with generous tracking; body copy should remain compact and highly readable.

### Brand Essence
A Sydney property styling service for sellers who want a more considered first impression without an upfront styling payment while the sale proceeds. Personality: considered, transparent, assured.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are calm and memorable. CTAs are direct but never pushy. Microcopy explains conditions plainly and avoids overpromising.

Example lines:
- “Your home sells. We succeed.”
- “Prepare your property to be seen at its best.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use a refined “S&S” monogram built from two offset vertical strokes and a shared horizontal baseline, paired with a simple Stage & Stay wordmark. It should feel like a small architectural publisher’s mark rather than a real estate badge.

### Signature Brand Color
Deep Garden Green — #183B36.
